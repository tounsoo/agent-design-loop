## For the review note

- **Crowding** (`after/vendor-harbor-and-pine--phone-dark.png`, `after/vendor-harbor-and-pine--phone-light.png`): On Harbor & Pine, the Open invoices table (four columns including a Status badge) is wider than the 390 px window. The Due column is cut off at the right edge: dates read 'No / 9, / 20' and 'De / 3, / 20', and the table runs past the page gutter, so the page scrolls sideways. The base fit all four columns, with ugly mid-word wraps. The change makes this worse, because a person can no longer read when an invoice is due without scrolling the table. Only the Harbor page has four columns, so only it is affected. Fix: Make the four-column table fit the phone width. Let the Invoice and Due cells wrap at word boundaries or keep them from breaking mid-word, shrink the Status badge, or stack each row's Status under its invoice number on a phone, so Due stays visible with no sideways scroll.
- **Hierarchy** (`after/vendor-harbor-and-pine--phone-dark.png`, `after/vendor-harbor-and-pine--phone-light.png`, `after/vendor-helios-freight--phone-dark.png`, `after/vendor-helios-freight--phone-light.png`, `after/vendor-nothing-open--phone-dark.png`, `after/vendor-nothing-open--phone-light.png`): The vendor facts (Remittance, Terms, Open total) are stacked as label-over-value pairs with little space between pairs, and the value weight matches the labels. The block takes up about a third of the first view, and the Open total, the key number, is not stronger than the email or terms. The headings 'Open invoices' and 'Approved' sit clearly below the title, so the hierarchy holds. This is polish only. Fix: Give the Open total more emphasis than the other facts, or set the facts in a quieter, denser block so the invoice tables lead the page.
