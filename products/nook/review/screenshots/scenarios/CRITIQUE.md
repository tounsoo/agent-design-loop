## For the review note

- **Hierarchy** (`rooms--desktop-light.png`, `rooms--desktop-dark.png`, `rooms--phone-light.png`, `rooms--phone-dark.png`, `waitlist--desktop-light.png`, `waitlist--desktop-dark.png`, `waitlist--phone-light.png`, `waitlist--phone-dark.png`, `waiting--desktop-light.png`, `waiting--phone-light.png`, `freed--desktop-light.png`, `freed--phone-dark.png`): Room names (Harbor, Lantern, Quarry, Cedar, Birch) are set in the same bold serif at about four fifths of the page title's size, so the list items nearly match 'Book a room' and 'Waitlist'. On the waitlist page the row names sit directly under the title with little step down. Fix: Drop the room name in a list to a clearly smaller step (a heading or label size) so the page title stays the strongest thing.
- **Grouping** (`waiting--desktop-light.png`, `waiting--phone-light.png`, `freed--desktop-light.png`, `freed--phone-dark.png`, `waitlist--desktop-dark.png`): The status banner ('You're on the waitlist for Cedar', 'Cedar is free now') sits almost touching the first list row and repeats what the row beneath it already shows (the room name plus Leave waitlist or Choose). The gap between banner and list is much smaller than the gap between header and banner. Fix: Give the banner the same spacing below it as above, and keep the banner to what the row can't say (for example, what to do next) rather than naming the room again.
- **Grouping** (`booking-harbor--desktop-light.png`, `booking-harbor--phone-light.png`, `booking-lantern--desktop-light.png`, `booking-quarry--phone-light.png`, `booked--desktop-light.png`, `held--desktop-light.png`, `expired--desktop-light.png`, `held--phone-light.png`, `held--desktop-dark.png`): On the booking page the 'All rooms' back link is indented from the title and details below it, so it looks misaligned. The content column also changes width between states (narrow with no banner, wider when a held or expired banner needs room), and the action buttons shift from left-aligned to right-aligned. The Hold and Book buttons jump around from state to state. Fix: Align the back link to the column's left edge, hold the column at one width in every state, and keep the action row at one alignment.
- **Grouping** (`booking-harbor--desktop-light.png`, `booked--desktop-light.png`, `held--desktop-light.png`, `expired--desktop-light.png`, `error--desktop-light.png`, `loading--desktop-light.png`): The banner on the booking pages sits tight above the 'How long?' label, as if it belonged to that field rather than to the page. In booked, error and loading states the 'Rooms' tab turns grey while the underline stays. Fix: Space the banner clear of the form field. Keep the active tab at full text weight in every state.
- **Crowding** (`booked--desktop-light.png`, `booked--phone-light.png`, `booked--desktop-dark.png`, `booked--phone-dark.png`): After booking, the full form stays on the page with the 'How long?' field and both Hold room and Book room shown disabled. Two dead buttons and a greyed field take up as much room as the confirmation. Fix: Once booked, hide or collapse the disabled form controls and let the confirmation banner lead.
- **Hierarchy** (`no-rooms--desktop-light.png`, `no-rooms--phone-light.png`): The empty state centers its heading, text and button under a left-aligned page title and subtitle. 'No rooms are free' repeats the subtitle's 'next hour and a half' wording in smaller text just below it. Fix: Left-align the empty state with the title, and trim the repeated sentence.
